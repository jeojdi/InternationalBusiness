STEP 1: First Contact

Key Actions:

  • →Use first names (low power distance)
  • →Be approachable, not hierarchical
  • →Come prepared with pricing/margins
  • →Show transparency about product sourcing

DO:

  • •Be punctual
  • •Use "G'day" greeting
  • •Focus on individual benefits
  • •Be authentic

DON'T:

  • •Overpromise
  • •Use complex jargon
  • •Arrive unprepared
  • •Be overly formal

Language Guide

"G'day!"

Universal Australian greeting - use it!

"Arvo"

Afternoon - casual, friendly

"Servo"

Gas station/petrol station - distribution point

"Biscuit" not "Cookie"

IMPORTANT: Tim Tams are biscuits in Australia!
